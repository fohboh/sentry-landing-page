The shift

An audit tells you what happened last year. Sentry tells you what's true, every cycle.

Periodic audit

  • Evidence assembled under time pressure, once a year
  • Findings are already historical by the time you see them
  • Each engagement re-litigates whether the process was followed correctly
  • A gap in one location's records stalls everything
  • Recovery windows with processors and platforms close while you wait

Rolling certification

  • Evidence collected and reconciled weekly or monthly, not once a year
  • Every location's Trust Score reflects the latest cycle
  • The same 198-rule deterministic pipeline runs every time — reproducible by design
  • A CAAR is ready the moment a period qualifies
  • Claims get filed inside the dispute window, with the evidence already sealed
What happens after you connect

You send data. Sentry does the rest.

Your part is one thing: connect the sources, once, with our team. Everything below runs automatically, every cycle, without anyone on your side touching it — and it runs the same way every time, which is what makes the output reproducible.

Sealed on arrival

Your data is hashed the moment it arrives, so what gets certified is provably what you sent.

Checked against a second source

POS against platform settlements, POS against bank deposits. A discrepancy has to appear in two systems to count.

Scored, then certified

198 deterministic rules run and a Trust Score is calculated. Below 85, nothing certifies — you get an itemized list of what's missing.

Delivered as evidence

A sealed CAAR, every dollar traced to the rule that found it. Ready to file, not to interpret.

Evidence standard

Built to hold up outside your building.

Recovery claims get challenged. The output is structured so the argument is about the money, not about how the number was produced.

FRE 803(6)

Business records exception

Certified values are written to an append-only Vault at the time they're generated — the standard for records made in the regular course of business.

FRE 902(11)

Self-authentication

Every CAAR is sealed with a SHA-256 hash computed against the final rendered document, so authenticity doesn't require a live witness.

FRE 1002

Best evidence rule

The same inputs reproduce the same hash, every time. No model, no inference, nothing to re-litigate about how a number was reached.
